Building upgrades

A look at every level, before any of this is wired into the game.

Preview only — the live game is still update 18. Nothing here has been added to play yet.

How upgrades work

  • Walk up and drop coins in the slot. A builder comes over and works on it, Kingdom-style.
  • Every building has three levels — wood, then stone, then iron — except tool stands, which do not upgrade.
  • Upgrades are expensive: 6, 12 and 20 coins.
  • The keep’s level caps every other building. Nothing can go above the keep.
  • Stone and iron come from mines you have to capture. Lose that land and you cannot start new upgrades of that material. Buildings you already upgraded stay as they are.

Every building, every level

Each row shows wood, stone and iron side by side, with the cost, what you get, and which mine you need.

Tower paths

A wood tower starts with one archer. At stone you pick a path — cannon or ballista — and iron makes that path stronger.

Mines

Each map hides a stone mine and an iron mine on land outside your starting ground. Capture the land to unlock that material.

A village on the frontier

A mixed village: an iron keep, upgraded houses and walls, both tower paths, and the two mines still out on wild land you would need to take.

Your land is the warm gold ground in the middle. The grey-green edges are still wild. Mines sit out there until you expand.

Tool stands are left out on purpose — they do not get upgrades. Farms, gates and captured forts are shown as proposals, since they already exist in the game.
